Information

how to flash
all tools
  1. Connect the Daisy Seed's micro-USB port straight to the computer (not a hub) with a data cable.
  2. Put the Seed in DFU mode: hold BOOT, press and release RESET, then let go of BOOT. The synth goes silent.
  3. Choose the firmware, click flash firmware and pick DFU in FS Mode in the popup.
  4. Wait for done. WEASEL restarts by itself; if not, press RESET once.

or, with firmware 0.2 or later running, skip the buttons:

Pick WEASEL's USB serial port in the popup. Your MIDI settings are kept; they live in the Seed's QSPI flash, which flashing never touches.

      Troubleshooting
      

        
DFU in FS Mode is not in the list

          
The Seed is not in DFU mode yet. Repeat step 2, try another cable (many are charge only) and plug straight into the computer. Restarting into DFU mode over USB also works if WEASEL is running firmware 0.2 or later.

        
Windows: "access denied" or "unable to claim interface"

          
Chrome can only reach the bootloader through the WinUSB driver. Get Zadig, put the Seed in DFU mode, choose Options › List All Devices, select DFU in FS Mode, pick WinUSB and click Replace Driver. Once per computer.

        
Linux: the device appears but can't be opened

          
Add a udev rule, for example SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="0483", ATTRS{idProduct}=="df11", MODE="0666" in /etc/udev/rules.d/50-stm32-dfu.rules, then unplug and replug.

        
"built for the Daisy bootloader"

          
That file was linked to run from the Seed's QSPI flash or SRAM through the Daisy bootloader. The ROM bootloader this page talks to can only write the 128 KB internal flash, so rebuild for internal flash.

        
It flashed but WEASEL doesn't start

          
Press RESET, or unplug and replug. If it still doesn't start, click download log and send it over.
