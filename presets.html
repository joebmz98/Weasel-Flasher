    Information

    what a preset holds

    • every mode the panel buttons step through › trigger selects, stages, envelope mode, waveforms, fm / am, polarity, midi, lo pass gate modes and clock mode
    • not the virtual patch bay, the knobs and faders, or the sequencer on / off switches › those stay as they are set on the panel

    how it works

    • click a mode › WEASEL changes straight away, like pressing its button
    • click a preset's name › recalls it; a lit dot marks the preset that matches the panel
    • presets are kept in this browser › export them to back up or move to another computer

    '*' next to a label is the mode WEASEL starts in
