    Information

    trigger modes

    • midi › triggered by a midi note
    • sequencer › triggered by each sequencer stage
    • pulsar › triggered by the pulsar
    • clock › triggered by the clock speed

    envelope generator modes

    • transient › sustains only for the sustain duration
    • sustain › sustains while the gate is held
    • oscillating › repeats like an oscillator

    dual lo pass gate modes

    • lo pass filter › modulation moves the cut-off
    • combination › moves the cut-off and the vca
    • voltage ctrld. amp › moves the vca

    the mod matrix

    • the centred sockets are modulation inputs; the numbered corner sockets are the four sources

    round buttons step through a section's modes, like the panel; '*' marks the mode WEASEL starts in
